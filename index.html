What's inside

Small cube. Real sensors.

AirENS161

Metal-oxide gas sensor for volatile organic compounds, with eCO₂ and TVOC estimates.

CO₂SCD41

True NDIR carbon dioxide on the Pro model, with its own temperature and humidity.

ClimateENS210

Temperature and relative humidity.

LightVCNL4040

Ambient light on the Pro model, so the glow dims itself at night.

BrainESP32‑H2

RISC-V module with Zigbee, Thread and Bluetooth LE. No Wi‑Fi, no cloud, no account.

Glow3 × RGB LEDs

Addressable IN‑PI15 LEDs light the diffused top: green, yellow or red.

PowerUSB‑C

5 V from any charger or computer. Over USB it also streams readings and 7 days of history.

Body49 × 49 × 32 mm

Five 3D-printed parts: diffused top, main tray, air wall, light wall and button cap.

noware · A Noso workspace noso.so ↗

noware builds on the open-source AirCube hardware by StuckAtPrototype (Apache‑2.0). The 3D model uses the project's enclosure STEP files and board layout; component CAD comes from Espressif, KiCad, E‑Switch and datasheet reconstructions as listed in the model provenance. The SCD41 and VCNL4040 bodies are illustrative boxes. Airflow and air-quality states are illustrations, not measurements.
